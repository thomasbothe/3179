How do Health Outcomes Differ Around the World?

Healthcare systems and life expectancy vary widely around the world. But how do differences in resources and behaviour shape people's health outcomes, and what role do they play in helping people live longer, healthier lives?

Healthcare Expenditure

Countries commit vastly different amounts to healthcare.
But does spending more translate into better health outcomes?

Alcohol Consumption

Alcohol consumption varies widely across countries, shaped by cultural norms, values, and lifestyles.

Life Expectancy

Life expectancy offers a broader picture of population health, reflecting differences in living conditions, healthcare systems and access to treatment. Comparing countries reveals striking differences in how long people live.

Hospitals

Access to hospitals can have a drastic impact on the quality of healthcare people receive. In particular, people living in rural areas have significantly fewer resources available to them.

Measles Vaccination

Longer lives depend not only on treating illness, but also on preventing it. Vaccination protects people from preventable diseases, but coverage varies between countries. Do gaps in protection correlate with lower heatlh outcomes?
